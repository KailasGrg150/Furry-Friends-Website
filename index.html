A little bit about us

At Furry Friends, we believe and prioritise bonds between humans and dogs. Through hours of training and countless assessments, we guarantee that our therapy dogs provide comfort, companionship, and emotional support to people of all ages.

Whether in schools, hospitals, care homes, or other environments, we ensure a safe and welcoming space where everyone feels comfortable. Together, we aim to make a positive impact and promote mental health support.

Benefits of dog therapy

Our team has gathered extensive knowledge on the importance of dog therapy through scientific research and real-life experiences.

Spending time with a therapy dog can reduce stress and anxiety, boost mood, and create a sense of comfort and relaxation—even in environments like hospitals and schools.

Over time, therapy dogs have made a significant impact on improving mental health.
