Help Us To Help Others


At Furry Friends, we are incredibly grateful for your support. Pets bring comfort, companionship and joy, and our therapy animals make a real difference in hospitals, schools and care homes by helping to reduce stress and improve wellbeing. Our visits help build confidence, encourage communication and create calm, welcoming environments for people of all ages, giving individuals something positive to look forward to.


Thanks to your support, we are able to train more volunteers, care for our animals and reach even more communities, continuing to bring comfort and positivity to those who need it most.
