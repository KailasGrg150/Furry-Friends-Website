Our Dogs

Meet some of our friendly and carefully trained therapy dogs, each selected for their calm temperament, gentle nature, and love of human interaction. These dogs play an important role in our visits to hospitals, schools, care homes, and community centres,where they help reduce stress, ease anxiety, and bring comfort to people of all ages. Through simple interactions like petting, sitting together, or going for short walks, our therapy dogs create meaningful connections that can brighten someone's day and promote a sense of companionship.
